Iqama — Support

Iqama shows the prayer times and counts down to the azan and the iqama. It runs on iPhone and Mac.

Contact

Send questions, bug reports, and feature requests to omar.tamimid1@gmail.com. You can also open an issue on GitHub.

Common questions

Where do the prayer times come from?
In the UAE, the app uses the official Awqaf times for each emirate. These times are in the app and need no network. In other countries, the app calculates the times for your city with the Aladhan service.

The times are not correct for my mosque.
Open Settings and change the iqama offset for each prayer. Friday has a separate setting.

I do not hear the Adhan.
On iPhone, the Adhan plays in the notification when the app is closed or in the background. Make sure that notifications for Iqama are on in iOS Settings, and that the Adhan is on for that prayer in the app.

The Live Activity does not show.
Make sure that Live Activities for Iqama are on in iOS Settings. Open the app once a day so that it can schedule the next prayers.
